**The main contributions of this study are summarized as follows:**

1. A progressive learning strategy is proposed to separate panicle localization from disease-category discrimination. It transfers localization knowledge from public single-class panicle datasets to rice white panicle detection, reducing the dependence on fully annotated binary datasets.
2. A detector–classifier cascade is developed to assign healthy and diseased categories to incompletely annotated field images. This strategy enables the construction of the WP_Dataset while reducing the amount of instance-level manual annotation.
